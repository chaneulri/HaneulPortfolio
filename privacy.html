Triton Schedule — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Triton Schedule is an unofficial browser extension that displays course schedule information from the UC San Diego Triton Student System (TSS) while you are signed in.

Data accessed

The extension reads course enrollments and related schedule information from TSS, including course titles, meeting days and times, classrooms, and instructors, to render the timetable.

How data is used

Schedule information is processed locally in your browser to show your calendar. The extension makes read-only requests to TSS using your existing authenticated session.

Data sharing and storage

The current extension source does not send your course information to external servers, use analytics, or persist it in Chrome extension storage. Schedule data is held temporarily in page memory while the extension runs.

Permissions

The extension operates only on https://tss.ucsd.edu/* and does not request additional Chrome API permissions.

Security

The extension does not ask for your TSS password. You sign in directly on the official TSS website. Your browser manages the authenticated session.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email littlemo.artstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

Any material changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy.

Triton Schedule is not affiliated with or endorsed by UC San Diego.
